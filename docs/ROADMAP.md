# Roadmap (beyond the StPT core)

Order and prerequisites as the owner set them. Nothing here is built or run until its own brief and gates exist.

1. **StPT core** (this repository): blind, live and display-pulse conditions; gates S0–S10.
2. **Rungs 1b, 1c, 2–4** as in the brief's study ladder: committed nodes, prerecorded boards and reversal branches;
   the Lucas test; the dose factor; the thinking kernel.
3. **Paper 1 causal replication on Qwen3.5-27B (added 2026-11-02, owner; coordination D24).** This is the prerequisite
   for the steer-one-agent diffusion study. Components:
   - a valence probe gate;
   - steering against an orthogonal placebo;
   - a method check.

   It runs on the shared pipeline (pinned), in its own pre-registered study with its own gates.
4. **Steer-one-agent diffusion study.** Starts only after item 3 passes: one steered agent on the board, and whether
   its steered votes diffuse.
